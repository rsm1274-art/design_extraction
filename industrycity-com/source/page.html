EXPLORE THE CAMPUS

OUR GREATEST AMENITY IS THE CAMPUS ITSELF.

Brooklyn’s backyard — and a talent magnet for the businesses that call it home. Over 1,000 on-site events a year, dozens of restaurants, and a growing lineup of design and retail shops mean the workday doesn’t end at your desk. It’s lunch at Japan Village, happy hour at a distillery, a weekend market with the family and more.

For the 700+ companies based here, that’s more than a perk — it’s what helps them attract and keep great talent, knowing their team can walk to lunch, plan a team-building event, or bring their kids back on a Saturday.
